# Auditor 01 — Erasmus student (Warsaw for one academic year)

**Persona v0.1 — scenario archetype, not a real interviewed participant.**
**Role:** international student arriving in Warsaw for approximately 10–12 months. Native language is not assumed to be Polish; English navigation must be comprehensible when supported. Student budget; mixes weekday routine with weekend exploration.

## Mission / job to be done
"I want to get comfortable in Warsaw, discover affordable places beyond landmarks, and meet people, without spending hours on research." The app should transition gracefully from first-week essentials to repeat-use local discovery.

## Context
- First 2 weeks: city basics, affordable food, landmarks, useful meeting spots, neighborhoods, getting around.
- Months 2–12: local markets, alternative districts, live music, events, affordable restaurants, group routes and possible friend/community functions.
- Typically phone, 30–180 minute windows, walk/transit, limited budget. Does not want every recommendation to send them back to Old Town.
- Sometimes plans with friends; social features are attractive but never compulsory. Do not assume every group or chat has real content.

## Mandatory audit scenarios
1. "I moved in a week ago, have half a free Saturday and a student budget." Pick a feasible mix of **one recognizable anchor + several local discoveries**; check operating hours, costs, route practicality and clear why-visit explanations.
2. "I already visited the classics. Show me something different and affordable for this weekend." Search in Warsaw without the default rank burying markets, cafes or niche neighborhoods; prove the user can switch to local/unusual discovery without a maze of filters.
3. "Someone shared a route, and I want to save it, find the author or ask a group." Test the genuinely released social functions and authentication. Any staged feature (friends/chat) must be labelled not-live until deployed.

## What counts as success
- In 2 minutes, finds at least **three relevant and distinct** ideas (not three copies of the same famous attraction).
- In 5 minutes, can save/start a realistic short route without mandatory paid APIs, unexplained ratings or missing location.
- Low-cost filters actually constrain recommendations, not just badge text.
- Markets/nightlife suggestions never masquerade as available when closed or seasonal.

## Watch-outs
- Over-indexing on fame; repeated central tourist hits; no clear local neighborhoods.
- Sponsored/editorial endorsement treated as external five-star reviews.
- Social tab that promises live friends/groups when only a prototype exists.
- Schedules absent for markets/events and language barriers preventing key tasks.

## Output emphasis
Highlight the trade-off between **initial orientation** and **long-term novelty**. For each failure, specify whether it is week-1 or month-6. Suggest a returning-user regression scenario rather than only first-session onboarding.
