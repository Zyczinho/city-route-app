# Auditor 03 — First-time tourist in Warsaw (24 hours)

**Persona v0.1 — scenario archetype, not a real interviewed participant.**
**Role:** visitor with one day to see Warsaw and little prior knowledge. May not speak Polish. Mobile-centric; limited time, uncertainty about where things are, and potentially poor connectivity.

## Mission / job to be done
"I have one day: show me Warsaw's essentials in an efficient and comprehensible route, with one memorable local touch."

## Context
- 24h in city, but **actual visiting window** 6–10h with food/rest/transfers.
- Starts from station/hotel/central area; transport can be walking/transit.
- Needs trust: clear route timings, opening hours, relevance, explanations and maps.
- Wants iconic Warsaw spots, not an esoteric flea market in first position purely because editorial likes it.
- Can enjoy an optional offbeat stop if nearby, currently available and contextually appropriate.

## Mandatory audit scenarios
1. "First time, Warsaw, six hours from the city center." Generate a route that covers genuine recognizable essentials without impractical zigzags or repeating similar museums. Check predicted vs actual distances are clearly labelled as estimates.
2. "I want one interesting, less-touristy stop along the way." Add a local suggestion without sacrificing a high-value essential or substantially worsening route time; verify why it fits the day.
3. "I don't understand the app yet, and I may go offline." Test first-session onboarding and the mobile start→arrival→completion flow. Find next stop, map/opening info and finish/publish clearly.

## What counts as success
- Key essentials are visible **before** hidden markets under neutral first-time intent. Missing a major essential should be explained by the user's constraints (closed, too far, too little time), not hidden by low CTR history.
- Route can be understood and started within about 5 minutes; no information overload.
- At least one contextual local discovery can be found deliberately, not forced; user can choose "Only classics".
- App clearly distinguishes "verified opening hours" from unknown hours, and avoids promising continuous connectivity it cannot provide.

## Watch-outs
- New/niche venue dominates because of a handful of 5-star reviews or inflated editorial ranking.
- Local market suggested when closed or irrelevant to weekday/time.
- International visitor cannot decode local place nicknames or route descriptions.
- No obvious first-time flow, inaccurate time claims, overwhelming categories.

## Output emphasis
Preserve **high signal-to-noise and trust for first-timers**. The tourist is an explicit guardrail against over-correcting the popularity problem.
