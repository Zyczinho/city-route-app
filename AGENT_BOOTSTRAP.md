Use this exact short command when starting/resuming an agent session:

> Sync latest `main`. Read `AGENT_COORDINATION.md`, `DECISIONS.md` and `ECONOMY_MODE.md`. Continue the task(s) assigned to you under `AGENTS.md`. Do not ask me unless RED-risk or genuinely blocked. Push/checkpoint before a session or credit limit.
