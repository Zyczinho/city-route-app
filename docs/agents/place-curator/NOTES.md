# Place Curator — notes

Short, dated lessons this role must know on its next run. Newest first. Update in the same PR as the work.

- 2026-11-06 — Warsaw OSM (#112): a quality gate (website/hours/social for venues, Wikidata for sights) drops ~1,150 candidates and brand tags plus a name list drop ~290 chains. Skip offices, embassies, faculties and stations by OSM tag, not by hand. Parks and palaces need an identical-name check within 1 km, because their OSM centre sits 150+ m from catalogue pins.
- 2026-11-05 — Wikimedia thumbnails are served from `thumb.wikimedia.org` (also `upload.wikimedia.org`); ported licences like `CC BY-SA 3.0 pl` are free.
- 2026-11-05 — Free photo coverage sample: Kraków 8/10, Warszawa 4/10 for landmarks; small cafés and bars almost never have a free photo.
- 2026-11-05 — OSM short names ("Charlotte") vs Google long names ("Charlotte. Chleb i Wino") match only within 100 m when every word of the shorter name is in the longer.
